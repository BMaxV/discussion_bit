0. about this document

This document has three stages.

  1. A description of the event or situation
    Including the names of people who were involved if available and specific examples of the problem.
  2. The specific rules or principles you feel were violated, with citation if possible. If no such rule exists, a general expression of unfairness or "not right"ness is good enough.
  3. Proposed or demanded changes or actions
This document is intended as a formalized, subjective perspective. Any descriptions or accusations or demands are opinions and not final and not proof read for legal or organizational compatibility. The dissonance between the legal situation and the actual situation, or organizational or official perspective, may be the problem and the cause of the complaint.
